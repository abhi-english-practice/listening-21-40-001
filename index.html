POP DOSSIER IELTS COLLOCATION LISTENING 21-40

21. Reliable colleague

Meaning: A coworker who can be trusted and depended on.
Vietnamese: đồng nghiệp đáng tin cậy
Example: She is a reliable colleague who always supports her team.

22. Highly qualified

Meaning: Having the necessary skills, education, or experience for a job.
Vietnamese: có trình độ cao
Example: The company is looking for highly qualified engineers.

23. Competent manager

Meaning: A manager who has the ability and skills to lead effectively.
Vietnamese: nhà quản lý có năng lực
Example: A competent manager can motivate employees.

24. Professional experience

Meaning: Knowledge and skills gained through working in a profession.
Vietnamese: kinh nghiệm chuyên môn
Example: This position requires professional experience.

25. Improve performance

Meaning: To make work, skills, or results better.
Vietnamese: cải thiện hiệu suất
Example: Employees should improve performance through training.

26. Evaluate performance

Meaning: To assess how well someone or something works.
Vietnamese: đánh giá hiệu suất
Example: Managers evaluate performance every year.

27. Meet growing demand

Meaning: To provide enough products or services for increasing needs.
Vietnamese: đáp ứng nhu cầu ngày càng tăng
Example: Companies must meet growing demand.

28. Generate revenue

Meaning: To produce income or money.
Vietnamese: tạo doanh thu
Example: Online businesses can generate revenue.

29. Boost sales

Meaning: To increase the number of products sold.
Vietnamese: tăng doanh số
Example: Advertising can boost sales.

30. Cut costs

Meaning: To reduce spending or expenses.
Vietnamese: cắt giảm chi phí
Example: Businesses try to cut costs.

31. Digital systems

Meaning: Computer-based systems that store and process information.
Vietnamese: hệ thống kỹ thuật số
Example: Digital systems improve efficiency.

32. Rely heavily on

Meaning: To depend strongly on something or someone.
Vietnamese: phụ thuộc nhiều vào
Example: Many companies rely heavily on technology.

33. Compatible with

Meaning: Able to work or exist successfully together.
Vietnamese: tương thích với
Example: This software is compatible with most devices.

34. Obsolete technology

Meaning: Old technology that is no longer useful or used.
Vietnamese: công nghệ lỗi thời
Example: Companies replace obsolete technology.

35. Preserve wildlife

Meaning: To protect wild animals and their habitats.
Vietnamese: bảo tồn động vật hoang dã
Example: National parks help preserve wildlife.

36. Scarce resources

Meaning: Resources that are limited and difficult to find.
Vietnamese: nguồn tài nguyên khan hiếm
Example: Water can become scarce resources in dry areas.

37. Abundant supply

Meaning: A large amount of something available.
Vietnamese: nguồn cung dồi dào
Example: The country has an abundant supply of natural resources.

38. Reduce costs

Meaning: To make expenses lower.
Vietnamese: giảm chi phí
Example: Automation can reduce costs.

39. Disrupt services

Meaning: To interrupt or disturb normal services.
Vietnamese: làm gián đoạn dịch vụ
Example: Storms can disrupt services.

40. Attract visitors

Meaning: To encourage people to come to a place.
Vietnamese: thu hút khách tham quan
Example: The museum uses events to attract visitors.
